Shetkari Bazaar - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Shetkari Bazaar collects the following information:

  • Phone number (for authentication)
  • Name and profile information (provided during onboarding)
  • Location data (to show nearby listings)
  • Listing photos and details (uploaded by users)
  • Device token (for push notifications)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and identify users
  • To display your listings to other users
  • To send relevant notifications about mandi rates and activity
  • To improve app functionality

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Database, Storage)
  • Truecaller (Phone verification)
  • Cloudinary (Image storage)
  • Google Maps (Location services)

5. Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and data by contacting us at: shetkari.bazaar.app@gmail.com

6. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: shetkari.bazaar.app@gmail.com
